RAAHI: Voice Counsellor for Skill Training in Indian Languages | SIH 2026 Demo

RAAHI is a phone call that helps people choose the right skill course. The caller speaks in their own language, RAAHI asks a few short questions, and then reads out the three courses that fit best, with the nearest training centre and its distance. The details are also sent by SMS or WhatsApp.

Schemes like PM-AJAY fund skill training, but people are rarely helped to choose a course that fits their education, their interests and the jobs in their district. Only about 43% of certified PMKVY 1.0 - 3.0 trainees were placed, and about 35 crore Indians still use feature phones that can't run an app. RAAHI works on any phone, in Indian languages.

How it works
- Sarvam speech-to-text turns the caller's speech into text
- Rules plus an LLM fill in the profile; each field is checked against what the caller actually said
- Skill words are translated to English and matched to courses ("बिजली का काम" leads to electrician courses)
- A fixed SQL score ranks courses: skill match 40%, local job demand 20%, level fit 15%, distance 15%, work preference 10%. The LLM never ranks courses, so the same answers always give the same result.
- Sarvam text-to-speech reads the result back in the caller's language

Built with: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js, Sarvam AI. Data: National Qualifications Register courses, training centres, district job openings and PM-AJAY rules, 770 districts loaded.

This is a working prototype. The phone line and WhatsApp are simulated in the demo, and centre and job data are partly sample data.
