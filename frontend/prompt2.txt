CORE INTERACTION: SCROLL-SCRUBBED IMAGE SEQUENCE
   A full-viewport HTML5 Canvas is pinned/sticky for the duration of the sequence (section height ≈ 500–600vh, so
there is room to scroll comfortably through 240 distinct frames without them flying past too fast).
  Frame index = a smooth, clamped function of scroll progress within that pinned section: frameIndex =
Math.floor(scrollProgress * 239). No easing lag on the frame draw itself — it should feel directly, physically tied
to the scrollbar, the way the wheel of a film reel is tied to your hand.
  Preload all 240 frames (or a resolution-appropriate set) before enabling scroll-scrub, with a lightweight loader
state, so there is no flicker or pop-in on first scroll.
  Scrolling down = the rider moves forward, the story advances toward the call. Scrolling up = the sequence plays
in reverse, exactly as filmed. The mechanic must feel reversible and scrubbable, not like a one-shot video.
  The canvas draws each JPG letterboxed/cropped to always fill the viewport at the source 800×450 aspect ratio,
centered, no distortion.
