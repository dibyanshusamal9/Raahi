About RAAHI · राही के बारे में

मदद हमेशा इतनी करीब थी।Help was always this close.

The government funds skill training for Scheduled Caste families through the Grant-in-Aid (GIA) component of PM-AJAY. But the very first step — building a profile of the person — assumes they can read, type, and name a career path from a list of bureaucratic categories. For a low-literacy beneficiary who may not own a smartphone, that first step is a locked door.

RAAHI is an AI that opens it with a conversation. It listens in the beneficiary’s own language and dialect over an ordinary phone call, a WhatsApp voice note, a lightweight app, or a village kiosk — then maps what it hears against government-recognised NSQF courses and the real livelihood demand around them. No literacy required. No app to install. No jargon to decode.

🎙️

यह सुनता हैIt listens

The beneficiary simply talks. Speech-to-text built for Indian languages captures the words; RAAHI never asks anyone to read or type.

🧠

यह समझता हैIt understands

An AI turns a messy, human sentence into a structured profile — family trade, education, mobility, interests, and whether they want a job or their own venture.

🎯

यह रास्ता दिखाता हैIt recommends

It replies out loud with something concrete: three NSQF courses that fit, the nearest centre offering them, and the live local demand for that trade.

Our direction · हमारा संकल्प

हम क्यों आगे बढ़ रहे हैंMission & Vision

A clear purpose for every conversation—and a future where livelihood guidance reaches everyone.

◎

हमारा मिशनOur Mission

To make livelihood discovery accessible, personal and evidence-led for rural Scheduled Caste communities. RAAHI listens in each beneficiary’s own language, understands their skills, interests and circumstances, and connects them with suitable NSQF-aligned training, nearby opportunities and practical support—without requiring literacy, an app or familiarity with government systems.

✦

हमारी दृष्टिOur Vision

A future where every person—regardless of language, literacy, device or location—can make an informed and dignified livelihood choice. We envision public skilling that responds to people’s real lives and local economies, so every training investment can become a credible pathway to sustainable household income.
